Animated WebP → WebGL2

Streams example-animated.webp, decodes complete frames with ImageDecoder, uploads each to a texture-array layer, then generates mipmaps and draws every layer.

One warmup is excluded. Fetch uses cache: no-store (not a guarantee of cold OS/network caches). Decode wait includes waiting for streamed bytes. Upload measures CPU submission, not GPU execution. GPU-complete includes a fence after mipmaps and drawing; polling adds latency. No gl.finish() in the measured path.

Serve this directory over localhost; ImageDecoder requires a supported browser and secure context.
