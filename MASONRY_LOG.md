# Ruled Out

- **CSS `:has()`, `:hover`, `:focus-within`, and `~` selectors in `static/gallery.css`**: Disabling `.gallery-card:hover` / `:focus-within` rules, `.card-actions:has(.card-action-popover:popover-open)`, `.gallery-card::after` + nested `:has(...)`, and `#filter-bar:has(...) ~ #photo-grid` rules did not change `bench/scroll_stutter_bench.lua` pass 1 performance (`fps=48.8`, `p50=16.7ms`, `p95=33.4ms`, `max=66.7ms`, `jitter=16.7ms`, `dropped=303`, `blanks=954` vs. baseline `fps=49.0`, `p50=16.7ms`, `p95=33.4ms`, `max=66.6ms`, `jitter=16.7ms`, `dropped=296`, `blanks=963`).
- **`port.scrollTop` assignment, `scrollPreview()`, `measureMasonry()`, and `showNearbyCards()` JS execution**: Per-frame instrumentation on `bench/scroll_stutter_bench.lua` pass 1 showed `scrollSet` (`p50=0.00ms`, `p95=0.10ms`, `max=0.20ms`), `scrollPreview` (`p50=0.10ms`, `p95=0.40ms`, `max=0.90ms`), `measureMasonry` (`p50=0.90ms`, `p95=1.20ms`, `max=2.50ms`), and `showNearbyCards` (`p50=0.20ms`, `p95=0.50ms`, `max=6.00ms`) total ~1.2ms at p50 and ~2.2ms at p95.
- **Benchmark observer (`booruPreviewSnapshot()` + `countBlanks()`) as sole cause of `>20ms` frames**: Combined `stepJs` (`snap` `p50=2.00ms`/`p95=2.50ms`/`max=8.60ms` + `blanks` `p50=2.00ms`/`p95=2.50ms`/`max=8.00ms`) measured `p50=4.10ms`, `p95=4.90ms`, `max=10.80ms`—below the `20ms` dropped-frame threshold on its own, leaving the majority of the `p95=33.4ms` / `max=66.7ms` frame time outside those measured JS functions.
- **Synchronous `<img>` decoding (`decoding="async"` absent)**: Adding `decoding="async"` to all `<img>` elements in `bench/scroll_stutter_bench.lua` did not eliminate pass 1 stutter (`fps=50.4`, `p50=16.7ms`, `p95=33.4ms`, `max=50.1ms`, `jitter=16.7ms`, `dropped=260`, `blanks=972`).
- **`requestBatch()` / `loadBatch()` as primary cause of steady pass-1 stutter**: Skipping `draw()` while keeping `requestBatch()` / `loadBatch()` active during `bench/scroll_stutter_bench.lua` pass 1 changed metrics from baseline (`fps=49.0`, `p95=33.4ms`, `jitter=16.7ms`, `dropped=296`) to `fps=59.5`, `p50=16.7ms`, `p95=16.8ms`, `jitter=0.1ms`, `dropped=12` (`max=83.3ms`).
- **`<video>` elements as primary cause of pass-1 stutter**: Replacing the 200 `<video preload="none">` elements with `<img>` in `bench/scroll_stutter_bench.lua` degraded pass 1 (`fps=42.6`, `p50=16.7ms`, `p95=33.4ms`, `max=83.3ms`, `jitter=16.7ms`, `dropped=472`, `blanks=20`).
- **`draw()` JS CPU execution and canvas resizing**: Profiling `draw()` during pass 1 (`fps=48.6`, `dropped=304`) showed `resizes=0` (`canvas=936x3780`), `drawCalls` (`p50=0`, `p95=15`, `max=18`), and total `draw()` CPU time of `p50=0.30ms`, `p95=0.60ms`, `max=4.70ms` (`strip` `p95=0.30ms`, `layouts` `p95=0.00ms`, `clear` `p95=0.10ms`, `loop` `p95=0.40ms`).
- **Empty `gl.clear()` calls and `load`/`loadBatch` `schedule()` triggers as primary bottleneck**: Skipping `gl.clear()` when `toDraw.length === 0`, ignoring `load` events for parked/clean images, and gating `loadBatch()` `schedule()` calls yielded `fps=50.2`, `p50=16.7ms`, `p95=33.4ms`, `max=66.7ms`, `jitter=16.7ms`, `dropped=262`, `blanks=980`.
- **7-viewport strip height (`h * 7`) as sole cause of `draw()` drops**: Shrinking `stripHeight` from `h * 7` (`936x3780`) to `h * 1` (`936x540`) alongside empty-clear skipping reduced `dropped` from `262` to `121` (`fps=55.6`, `p50=16.7ms`, `p95=33.3ms`, `max=50.0ms`, `jitter=16.6ms`, `blanks=1248`), leaving `121` dropped frames.
